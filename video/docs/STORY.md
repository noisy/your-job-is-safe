# Story plan (style-independent draft for Phase 2)

What each part of the video must show, anchored to the aligned lyrics (`data/lyrics.json`) and the bar
grid (`data/audio.json`; 172 BPM, 1.39 s bars). Times are approximate; scenes look them up by content.
The style bible, idioms and morphs get added once a style is chosen. Source for every fact: `../../handoff.md`.

Principles: the lyrics wink, **the picture shows what actually happened**; the viewer sees what the
narrator refuses to see (dramatic irony that grows chorus by chorus); the final chorus mirrors the
first with the same images turned against him; the robot arrives exactly on the 3:12.45 doorbell.

The narrator: a smug programmer (our own design, stylised: hoodie, headphones round the neck, mug with
a semicolon on it). Never a real person. Real people and brands only as plain text labels.
